## 1. What Is Google Ads?

Google Ads is an online advertising platform that helps businesses display advertisements across eligible Google advertising networks.

Businesses can use Google Ads to reach relevant audiences based on factors such as search queries, location, audience signals and campaign settings.

## 2. Define Your Advertising Goals

Before launching a campaign, decide what you want to achieve:

- Generate business enquiries.
- Receive phone calls.
- Increase website visits.
- Promote products or services.
- Generate online sales.

Choose campaign goals that match your actual business objectives.

## 3. Conduct Keyword Research

- Identify relevant search terms.
- Understand customer search intent.
- Use specific and relevant keywords.
- Review search terms regularly.
- Add irrelevant queries as negative keywords when appropriate.

## 4. Create Relevant Advertisements

- Write clear and accurate ad headlines.
- Explain the actual service or product.
- Highlight relevant benefits.
- Use suitable calls to action.
- Match advertisements with their landing pages.

## 5. Optimize Landing Pages

A useful landing page should:

- Explain the advertised service clearly.
- Load properly on mobile devices.
- Include relevant contact information.
- Have a clear enquiry or conversion action.
- Match the promise made in the advertisement.

## 6. Manage Your Advertising Budget

- Set a budget based on business capacity.
- Monitor advertising expenditure.
- Review campaign performance.
- Avoid making decisions from short-term fluctuations alone.
- Keep advertising spend separate from agency management fees.

## 7. Measure Campaign Performance

Review relevant metrics such as:

- Impressions
- Clicks
- Click-through rate
- Average cost per click
- Conversions
- Cost per conversion

Interpret these metrics in the context of business enquiries and sales.

## 8. Common Google Ads Mistakes

- Using irrelevant keywords.
- Sending traffic to unrelated pages.
- Ignoring search terms.
- Not tracking conversions correctly.
- Making frequent changes without enough data.
- Ignoring mobile landing page experience.
