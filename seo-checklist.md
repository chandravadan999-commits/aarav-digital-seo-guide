
# Website SEO Checklist

A practical SEO checklist for website owners and small businesses.

Prepared by Aarav Digital Marketing Agency, Ahmedabad, India.

Website: https://aaravdigital.in/

## 1. On-Page SEO

- Write a unique title tag for every important page.
- Create a clear and relevant meta description.
- Use one descriptive H1 heading.
- Organize content with relevant H2 and H3 headings.
- Use meaningful URLs.
- Add descriptive image alt text.
- Link relevant pages internally.

## 2. Technical SEO

- Make sure important pages return HTTP 200.
- Check that important pages are not blocked by robots.txt.
- Use the correct canonical URL.
- Create and maintain an XML sitemap.
- Check mobile usability.
- Use HTTPS.
- Fix broken internal links and redirect errors.

## 3. Local SEO

- Keep business name, address and phone number consistent.
- Maintain an accurate Google Business Profile.
- Add relevant business categories and services.
- Collect genuine customer reviews.
- Keep business contact information updated.

## 4. Content Quality

- Answer real customer questions.
- Publish useful and original content.
- Support factual claims with reliable information.
- Update outdated information.
- Avoid keyword stuffing and duplicate content.

## 5. Measurement

- Monitor Google Search Console.
- Review organic clicks and impressions.
- Track important landing pages.
- Check indexing issues.
- Review conversions and enquiries.
